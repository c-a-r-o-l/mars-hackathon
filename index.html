Mars Trip Checker

The settlement's delivery dispatcher — demo

Will the delivery get there?

Tell the dispatcher about the trip. It checks every rover in the fleet and tells you which one should go — and whether the trip will succeed.

What's the point?

  • Today, 1 in 3 delivery trips fails — mostly because the wrong rover gets sent.
  • The dispatcher fixes that: it re-planned all 1,500 trips in the settlement's logbook and the success rate went from 63.9% to 87.1% simulation
  • It moved 324 trips off the little swarm robot (which failed 4 out of 5 trips) onto rovers that can actually finish them.
  • For 6 trips, no rover can do the job — the dispatcher says no instead of sending a rover out to fail.

How it was built

  • Taught a model to guess how much battery a trip uses, from 1,500 example trips in the settlement's logbook.
  • It's right to within about 190 watt-hours on trips it has never seen before.
  • Found the exact rule the logbook uses to count a trip as a success: more than 5% battery left, and the route must be safe.
  • Dispatch rule: send the smallest rover that can carry the load and finish with battery to spare.
  • The 87.1% comes from re-planning the same 1,500 trips in simulation — the alternative assignments were never really driven.
