TRACK C  -  Life Support  -  ESA HREDA / Mars500 closed-loop pack
=============================================================

What this is for
  Closed-loop O2 / water / food / waste forecasting and greenhouse air
  ventilation for a 100-person settlement across a minimum 730-day window,
  including a dust-storm season that crashes solar availability.

Source dataset
  ESA HREDA archive (Mars500 / Mars105 ECLSS + physiological telemetry framing)
  http://esdcdoi.esac.esa.int/doi/html/data/hre/hreda/8b3a6c3f-e7a0-4fb1-8693-8edd6515d06d.html
  Metabolic rate priors: NASA ECLSS / Weiland (0.84 kg O2 per person-day, etc.)

What we sliced / simulated
  SIMULATED (HREDA/Mars500-informed), scaled to hack preconditions.
  Public HREDA bulk telemetry is archive-gated for many users, so this pack
  builds a clean daily ECLSS table using Mars500 closed-loop experiment
  structure + published per-person rates, then scales to crew_size=100 over
  730 days.

  Dust storm days 180-260: lower solar_availability,
  slightly worse recovery efficiency, higher greenhouse_ventilation_m3_per_h
  and cabin_co2_ppm_proxy (indoor air stress).

Files
  mars500_eclss_scaled_100p_730d.csv
  window_totals.json
  load_example.py
  manifest.json

How to use tonight
  Forecast resource margins or tune a greenhouse HVAC brain off
  activity_level, dust_storm_flag, and ventilation columns.
