Track A · Architecture (EMARS climate prior)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Dataset: Ensemble Mars Atmosphere Reanalysis System (EMARS) Version 1.0
Authors: Greybush, S. J.; Kalnay, E.; Wilson, R. J.; Hoffman, R. N.; and coauthors
Paper: Greybush et al., Geoscience Data Journal
  https://doi.org/10.1002/gdj3.77
Archive DOI: https://doi.org/10.18113/D3W375
Landing page:
  https://www.datacommons.psu.edu/download/meteorology/greybush/emars-1p0/a_landing_page.html

What this repo ships: EMARS-INFORMED SIMULATION at one settlement site
(not a raw NetCDF extract). Full EMARS files are multi-GB.

License note: Penn State Data Commons listing does not show a clear
machine-readable open license on the landing page. Cite EMARS; do not claim
this pack redistributes the official NetCDF archive.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Track B · Vehicles & Mobility (AI4MARS terrain)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Dataset: AI4MARS — A Dataset for Terrain-Aware Autonomous Driving on Mars
Authors / custodians: Swan, R.; Ono, M.; Atha, D. (JPL / NASA)
NASA portal:
  https://data.nasa.gov/dataset/ai4mars-a-dataset-for-terrain-aware-autonomous-driving-on-mars
Zenodo: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.15995035
Related papers: IEEE Aerospace / CVPRW AI4MARS publications (cite per Zenodo note)

Mirror used for slicing (Hugging Face):
  https://huggingface.co/datasets/hassanjbara/AI4MARS
  (community mirror of the Kaggle / AI4MARS release; not an official NASA host)

What this repo ships:
  - 48 resized real image+label pairs sliced from the HF mirror
  - 12 synthetic dust-augmented frames (original to this pack)
  - logistics_duty_cycle_100p_730sol.csv (SIMULATED timetable for the hack)

License note: NASA Open Data Portal currently lists "License not specified"
for AI4MARS. Zenodo asks users to cite the related papers. Treat images as
NASA/JPL research data with attribution required; check upstream before
commercial reuse.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Track C · Life Support (ESA HREDA / Mars500 framing)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Archive framing: ESA Human and Robotic Exploration Data Archive (HREDA)
Portal: https://www.cosmos.esa.int/web/hreda
Example Mars500 investigation DOI page (framing only):
  http://esdcdoi.esac.esa.int/doi/html/data/hre/hreda/8b3a6c3f-e7a0-4fb1-8693-8edd6515d06d.html

Metabolic rate priors used in the simulated table (not HREDA telemetry):
  NASA NTRS 20170007268 summarizing Weiland (1994) baselines
  (~0.84 kg O2 / person-day and related ECLSS flows)

What this repo ships: SIMULATED daily ECLSS table scaled to 100 people /
730 days. No raw Mars500 personal / medical telemetry is included.

License note: ESA Space Science Archive data are commonly distributed under
CC BY-NC 3.0 IGO (attribution; non-commercial without prior ESA authorisation).
HREDA downloads require authentication even for public science data. This pack
does not redistribute ESA archive files.
