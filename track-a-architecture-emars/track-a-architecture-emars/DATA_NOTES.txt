TRACK A  -  Architecture  -  EMARS weather pack
===========================================

What this is for
  Thermal & weather-aware habitat design for a 100-person settlement across
  a 2 Earth-year (~730 sol) window, including one dust-storm season.

Source dataset
  EMARS (Ensemble Mars Atmosphere Reanalysis System) v1.0
  Paper: https://rmets.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/gdj3.77
  Archive: Penn State Data Commons (doi:10.18113/D3W375)
  Full EMARS spans Mars Years 24-33 as hourly NetCDF grids (~GB per Ls slice).

What we sliced / simulated
  SIMULATED (EMARS-informed), not a raw NetCDF extract.
  Full EMARS analysis files are multi-GB each, so this pack reconstructs an
  EMARS-style hourly surface time series at ONE settlement site using
  published Mars climate ranges (diurnal swing, thin-CO2 pressure ~610 Pa,
  dust optical depth, global-storm opacity spikes).

  Site: lat 18.4, lon 77.5 (Jezero-band proxy)
  Window: sols 0..729 (730 sols, 24 hours/sol)
  Dust storm season: sols 180-260 (dust_storm_flag=1)
  Habitat column: habitat_thermal_load_kw_100p scales heating demand to
  100 occupants (higher under dust / cold nights).

Files
  emars_settlement_hourly_730sol.csv / .parquet
      Hourly: temperature_c, wind_u_ms, wind_v_ms, surface_pressure_pa,
      dust_optical_depth, dust_storm_flag, habitat_thermal_load_kw_100p
  emars_settlement_daily_summary.csv
      Quick daily min/max/mean for architecture prototyping
  manifest.json
      Machine-readable provenance

How to use tonight
  Load the hourly CSV/parquet. Drive wall thickness, insulation, or a
  storm-bunker restaurant seal schedule off dust_storm_flag + thermal load.
  Cite EMARS as the climate prior; treat this file as the hack-ready slice.
