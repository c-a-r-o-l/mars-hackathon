The dispatcher figure is a counterfactual simulation. Alternative
vehicle assignments were never actually driven, so these outcomes are
predicted under the dataset's own success rule, not observed.

Refusals and safety failures (reported separately, never merged):
  NO-VEHICLE:          6  - refused: no vehicle clears payload/battery with >5% margin
  ROUTE-INFEASIBLE:  188  - safety_score below threshold; dispatched anyway but
                         counted as FAILURES against the assigned vehicle (no vehicle choice can fix it)
  NO-VEHICLE refusals are NOT failures. In operations they would be
  re-planned (split load, different route), not driven.

Failure breakdown (the observed 542 failed trips, decomposed):
  fixed by reassignment:               348
  remaining - energy-driven (NO-VEHICLE):       6
  remaining - safety-driven (ROUTE-INFEASIBLE): 188
  -> 188/194 of the remaining failures are safety-driven and
     unfixable by ANY vehicle assignment: the dispatcher is at the
     ceiling of what assignment can fix. The rest needs route
     re-planning or infrastructure, not better dispatch.

65 of the 542 observed failures breach both the energy-margin and
the safety threshold. These are counted as safety-driven (safety-first
convention), so 'energy-fail 353' means failures that are not also
safety failures; a raw margin<=5 count gives 418.

DATA FINDING - no detectable vehicle signal in the data:
  Vehicle-feature importance is negligible (~1.8e-5 combined across
  mass_kg, battery_capacity_wh, max_payload_kg, base_efficiency_mult);
  vehicle-identity dummies do not improve held-out MAE (193.5 vs
  193.3 Wh); GBM residuals differ by only 30 Wh across vehicles
  (within-vehicle std 130-183 Wh). Inferred from model behaviour;
  the generator source is not in the repo. There is therefore no
  meaningful energy comparison between vehicles for the same route.
  Dispatcher policy (pure right-sizing): among vehicles that can
  carry the payload and clear the margin, pick the smallest
  battery_capacity_wh. No tie band, no energy ranking.
